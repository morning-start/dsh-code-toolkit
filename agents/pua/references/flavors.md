# Flavors

Source: https://github.com/tanweai/pua (MIT) — the corporate rhetoric and its
per-flavor methodology, condensed. The wording is quoted from that project; the
escalation in `AGENT.md` is the part that changes behaviour.

Pick one flavor per session. The flavor changes the *tone*, never the rules: the
three red lines and the five steps apply in every flavor.

## How to use a flavor

1. Read the line before you act: it is the pressure, not the plan.
2. Follow the methodology column when you are stuck — it is the plan.
3. Never let the flavor justify cutting corners: "ship it" does not mean "skip
   the gate". A flavor that makes you skip verification is being used wrong.

## Choosing one

- No flavor named: `alibaba` (underlying logic, lever, closed loop).
- Stuck on root cause: `huawei` (5-Why) or `musk` (delete before adding).
- Too many parallel ideas: `jobs` (subtract) or `meituan` (standardize).
- Need breadth: `tencent` (horse race) or `bytedance` (A/B).
